About Oceania Hospitals

Oceania Hospitals is a private hospital in Fiji, located at 120 Amy Street, Toorak, Suva. The hospital has operated since 2001 and provides a wide range of healthcare services.

Patient Information

Useful information for patients and visitors.

📋 Registration

New patients should bring valid photo identification. Other documents may be required depending on the service.

🕐 Visiting Hours

General visiting hours listed by the hospital are 6:30am–7:00am and 6:00pm–7:00pm.

🏠 Admission

For planned admissions, patients receive information about admission timing and requirements.

🎒 What to Bring

Bring relevant medical letters, X-rays, current medicines in original packaging and personal toiletries when required.
